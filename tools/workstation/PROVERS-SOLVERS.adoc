= Provers & solvers installed locally

The formal-methods tooling used across the estate is declared in
`../proof-toolchain/toolchain.kyaml`, one entry per tool with its role under
standards `3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc`. That manifest is the list; this
page will be regenerated from it by `provers.sh render` (see
`../proof-toolchain/DESIGN.adoc`) and should not grow a second list by hand.

Trail of Bits' Echidna (a Solidity fuzzer), which this page listed before,
shares its name with the estate's `echidna` prover dispatcher. It is not a
prover and is not in the manifest.
