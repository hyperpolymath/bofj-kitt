Format note: where this document names an `.a2ml` file it is locating
today's content. A2ML is retired in favour of the deed format (grammar:
standards `1-formats/deed/spec/abnf/deed.abnf`) and `coordination.k9` by
k9-coordination (`2-protocols/k9-coordination/spec/abnf/coordination-k9-grammar_v1.2.abnf`);
ruling D313 makes the `.a2ml` to `.deed` conversion a tooling front end
rather than a hand sweep. Every new field proposed here (the registry's
`binary`, `family` and `owner` fields, the capability record's home)
targets the deed, not A2ML.

Status: proposal, 2026-10-27. Companion to
`environment-of-environments.adoc` (the capability record these cartridges
advertise from) and `awesome-estate-maths-proving.adoc` (the roster they
expose). The store state below was read from `hyperpolymath/boj-server-cartridges`
and the BoJ stdio bridge on that date.

== The gap, confirmed

Nothing public gives an agent MCP access to a *roster* of provers and
solvers. What exists upstream is per-tool: a Lean language-server MCP, a
handful of single-solver wrappers. The estate is the only place with a
141-variant dispatcher (echidna), a declared toolchain (this kit), and a
cartridge store with a formal-verification domain. The pieces exist; they
are not joined.

[cols="2,3,2",options="header"]
|===
|Surface |What it does today |Why it is not the answer

|`proof-mcp` (store, `domains/formal-verification`)
|Eight session-lifecycle tools over a state machine (idle, loading,
verifying, verified, failed) for seven backends: Lean, Coq, Agda, Isabelle,
Idris2, Z3, CVC5. Idris2 ABI with proofs; Zig FFI and adapter.
|Its adapter answers every tool with `"<tool> forwarded"` and its `mod.js`
posts to a REST backend on port 7707 that nothing serves. The store lists
it as `available: true`, which is a catalogue claim, not a running prover.
Seven backends is a hand-typed list.

|`echidna-llm-mcp` (store)
|Routes `consult` and `suggest_tactics` to an LLM.
|Advice, not proof. Opt-in by API key; must stay separate from anything
that returns a verdict.

|`proof-lsp` (store)
|IDE integration: diagnostics, hover, completion for four assistants.
|Editor protocol, not dispatch.

|`toolchain-mcp` (store, `domains/languages`)
|Mints and provisions LSP, DAP, BSP and language servers per workspace.
|Language toolchains, not provers; but its mint/provision/status shape is
the right model for provisioning a prover set from the kit's bundle.

|`crates/echidna-mcp` (echidna repository)
|A standalone `rmcp` server with three tools: `prove` (file, prover,
timeout), `check_prover`, `list_provers`. Shells out to the `echidna`
binary.
|It is the right backend and the wrong front door: BoJ ADR-0002 rules out
standalone MCP servers in the estate, so this surface must enter BoJ as a
cartridge. Its tool descriptions also hard-code "105 provers", the
drift `PROVER_COUNT.adoc` warns against.

|BoJ bridge (live)
|`boj_health` reports `rest-unavailable`, stdio-only, pending the Elixir
rewrite. Catalogue tools answer from a static manifest.
|Any cartridge built now must work through the stdio bridge and the
ADR-0006 `boj_cartridge_invoke` ABI, not through REST.
|===

== The proposal: three cartridges on one seam

Three cartridges, cut by *what the input is and what the answer means*,
not by which binary runs. They share one seam: the capability record from
`environment-of-environments.adoc` (E2), which says what the host can
actually dispatch, so no cartridge ever advertises a tool it cannot run.

=== `provers-mcp`: proving

Input is a proof artefact (a `.agda`, `.v`, `.lean`, `.thy`, `.idr`, `.p`,
`.mm` file or a goal string); the answer is a verdict with a trust level
and, where the backend produces one, a certificate.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Tool |Contract

|`provers_list`
|The provers this host can dispatch, read from the capability record
(`PRESENT` entries only), each with its echidna `ProverKind` slug, role
under PROOF-POLICY, version and lock hash. Never a hard-coded count.

|`provers_check`
|One prover: present, version, drift against the lock, or the reason it
is unavailable.

|`provers_prove`
|`{file \| goal, prover?, timeout}` → `{verdict, trust_level, prover,
certificate?, axioms, raw}`. Dispatches through `echidna prove --format
json`; the five-level trust hierarchy and the axiom scan are echidna's.

|`provers_portfolio`
|Same input, two or more provers; returns each verdict and the combined
trust level (level 5 only when two independent small-kernel systems agree
with certificates, per echidna's `confidence.rs`).

|`provers_recheck`
|The PROOF-POLICY §2 route: an Agda proof transported through Dedukti to
Rocq and Lean, with `transported` or `re-proved` recorded per leg and the
statement comparison named. Needs the kit's `transport` entries present.

|`provers_exchange`
|Translate between Dedukti, Lambdapi, TPTP, SMT-LIB, OpenTheory, SMTCoq
through echidna's exchange layer. No verdict; a translation and its hash.

|`provers_certificate`
|Check a certificate (Alethe, DRAT, LRAT, TSTP, Lean or Rocq kernel
export) independently of the prover that produced it.
|===

=== `solvers-mcp`: solving

Input is a problem (SMT-LIB, DIMACS, MiniZinc, LP, a TLA+ or Promela
model); the answer is a model, a proof of unsatisfiability, an optimum, or
a bounded model-checking result with its bounds. Solver results are
`TESTED` with bounds or `PROVEN` with a checked certificate, never
silently the latter.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Tool |Contract

|`solvers_list`, `solvers_check`
|As for provers, over echidna tiers 8, 9 and 10 and the kit's `backend`
entries (Z3, CVC5, Yices, Alt-Ergo, E, Vampire).

|`solvers_sat`
|`{problem, format, solver?, want: model \| unsat_core \| certificate}` →
the requested artefact plus timing. Portfolio across Z3, CVC5 and Alt-Ergo
with disagreement flagged, as echidna's `portfolio.rs` does.

|`solvers_optimise`
|MiniZinc, Chuffed, OR-Tools, GLPK, SCIP: objective, bound, solution,
proof of optimality where the solver emits one.

|`solvers_model_check`
|TLC, SPIN, NuSMV, Alloy, PRISM, Storm, UPPAAL, ProB: the property, the
bounds, the counter-example if any. Reported as `TESTED` with bounds, per
PROOF-POLICY §4.

|`solvers_certificate`
|Alethe, DRAT, LRAT, LFSC checking, shared with `provers_certificate`
through one implementation.
|===

=== `code-verifiers-mcp`: verifying code in its own language

Input is a source tree with contracts; the answer is a proof ledger in
the PROOF-POLICY §5 vocabulary (`PROVEN`, `PROVEN-WITH-AXIOMS`, `TESTED`,
`ASSUMED`, `OPEN`) with a receipt. This is the cartridge for the tools that
sit *on* code rather than on proof files: Creusot and Kani for Rust, SPARK
for Ada, Aeneas for Rust-to-model, Dafny, Why3 and Cameleer, F*, and the
estate's own verifiers as they arrive: Quintessence (Elixir/OTP, three
layers, already producing TrustLevel 4 certificates), Apodeixis.jl (Julia;
today a fail-closed policy audit, verifier planned), atoma and bactarin
(no initialised repository yet; atoma is still the untouched template and
bactarin has no repository in the estate listing).

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Tool |Contract

|`verifiers_list`
|The language-to-verifier mapping this host can serve, from PROOF-POLICY
§1 and the capability record: Rust → Creusot (+ Kani, Aeneas), Ada → SPARK,
Elixir → Quintessence, Julia → Apodeixis.jl, and the §4 exceptions.

|`verifiers_verify`
|`{path, language?, tool?, obligations?}` → the ledger: one row per
obligation with status, tool, version, provers used, axioms, and the
`ASSUMED` boundary rows for third-party code (§1.1).

|`verifiers_obligations`
|List the verification conditions a tool generates for a path, without
discharging them. Lets an agent see the proof surface before paying for it.

|`verifiers_receipt`
|The hashed receipt for a ledger: inputs, tool and prover versions by lock
hash, bounds for `TESTED` rows. Quintessence's SHA-256 binary receipt and
Apodeixis's planned warrant-to-receipt pipeline are the two estate
instances of this shape; the cartridge defines the common one.

|`verifiers_mutate`
|Run echidna's mutation engine (or Quintessence's six-kind one) over a
specification and report the mutation score against the 95% default.
|===

Why a third cartridge rather than a section of `provers-mcp`: the input
is code, the mapping is by language, the output is a ledger rather than a
verdict, and the future estate tools all land here. Keeping it separate
means Quintessence, Apodeixis.jl, atoma and bactarin each add one
`verifiers_list` row and one dispatch arm, with no change to the proving
or solving surfaces.

=== What `proof-mcp` becomes

Keep it, and make it honest: `proof-mcp` is the *lifecycle* layer (slots,
state machine, the Idris2 proofs it already has); its `proof_verify`
composes `provers_prove` under ADR-0014's composition contract instead of
posting to a port nothing serves. Its backend list is replaced by a read of
`provers_list`. The alternative, deleting it, throws away a proved state
machine for no gain.

== Options compared

[cols="1,3,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Option |Shape |Elegance / correctness |Dependability |Security / privacy

|*A. Three cartridges on one seam* (recommended)
|`provers-mcp`, `solvers-mcp`, `code-verifiers-mcp`; `proof-mcp` as
lifecycle over them; all advertise from the capability record.
|High. Each surface has one input kind and one answer kind; the roster is
never typed by hand.
|High once the kit's `doctor` exists; until then the cartridges can run
against echidna's own `check-prover`.
|High. Shield tier; sandboxed execution (echidna's Podman or bwrap
executor, ADR-0009's local provider); no LLM in the verdict path.

|*B. One `echidna-mcp` cartridge*
|Port the three tools of `crates/echidna-mcp` into the store as they are.
|Low. Satisfies ADR-0002 in an afternoon, but "prove this file with this
prover" is the whole surface: no portfolio, no ledger, no solving, and the
hard-coded "105".
|Medium. Nothing to maintain beyond echidna.
|Medium. Inherits echidna's sandbox only if `echidna prove` is called with
it on.

|*C. One `formal-methods-mcp` mega-cartridge*
|Every tool of A in one cartridge.
|Low. One `cartridge.json` with twenty tools across three answer kinds;
the ledger and verdict vocabularies get mixed.
|Medium.
|Medium. One tier for tools with very different blast radius.

|*D. Alternative path: the store is the manifest*
|Render each cartridge's `cartridge.json` tool catalogue and the `prover`
enum in its input schemas from echidna's `provers.a2ml` and the kit's
capability record, with one `invoke(prover, input)` tool per cartridge
whose schema is generated. Same `render` pattern as `provers.sh render`.
|Highest for drift (a new `ProverKind` variant appears in the schema on
the next render) and lowest for readability of the tool list.
|Medium until `provers.a2ml` carries the `binary` and `family` fields the
awesome-lists proposal asks for.
|High. Same as A.
|===

Recommendation: A, with D's generated `prover` enum adopted inside A's
input schemas as soon as the registry fields exist. B is the right
*first step* of A, not an alternative to it: port `crates/echidna-mcp`
into `provers-mcp` first, then grow.

== Experiments before building

[cols="1,3,2",options="header"]
|===
|Id |Experiment |Pass criterion

|Z1
|Run `crates/echidna-mcp` over stdio against a host with only Z3 present;
call `list_provers`, `check_prover`, `prove` on a one-line SMT-LIB file.
|`prove` returns `verified: true` for a satisfiable goal; `check_prover`
says `Lean` is unavailable rather than failing. This is the backend
contract the cartridge wraps.

|Z2
|Replace `proof-mcp`'s `mod.js` POST to port 7707 with a subprocess call
to `echidna prove --format json`, and call `proof_verify` through the

|The verdict reaches the caller; the Zig adapter's `"forwarded"` stub is
retired. Proves the cartridge can be real without the Elixir REST port.

|Z3
|Scaffold `provers-mcp` from `templates/gossamer-mcp`, write its
`cartridge.json`, run the store's `just validate` and
`scripts/typecheck-proofs.sh`.
|Manifest validates against `cartridge-v1.json` at the pinned SHA; the
Idris2 ABI type-checks with zero `believe_me`.

|Z4
|Feed the same theorem to Z3 and CVC5, then to Lean and Rocq, through
`provers_portfolio`.
|The SMT pair reports level 3 at most; the kernel pair reports level 5

|Z5
|Compose `proof-mcp.proof_verify` → `provers-mcp.provers_prove` and run
the ADR-0014 composition check on the typed boundary.
|The composition is admitted by the typed contract or the ADR names what

|Z6
|`verifiers_verify` on Quintessence's own 15-case suite and on a Creusot
example crate.
|Both produce a ledger in the §5 vocabulary with a receipt; the Creusot
rows name Why3 and the provers by lock hash.
|===

== The formal route

Three things here are interface contracts, so under PROOF-POLICY §1 they
are Idris2:

* The `prover` argument's type. Derive it from echidna's `ProverKind` (the
  `.machine_readable/provers.a2ml` slugs) so an unknown prover is rejected
  at the ABI boundary, not discovered as a runtime string mismatch. The
  cartridge template already puts the ABI in Idris2.
* The capability record, shared with `environment-of-environments.adoc`:
  one `EnvCapability` type consumed by `provers_list`, `solvers_list` and
  `verifiers_list`, so the three cartridges cannot disagree about what the
  host holds.
* The ledger row. The §5 vocabulary is a closed sum type; a `TESTED` row
  carries bounds and a `PROVEN` row carries a certificate reference by
  construction.

One theory statement is worth an Agda file: echidna's five trust levels
form a chain, and a portfolio's level is the meet of its members' levels
except for the one case (two independent small-kernel systems, both with
certificates) that lifts to level 5. Stating that as a lattice property and
rechecking it in Rocq and Lean per §2 is small, and it is the property an
agent relies on when it reads `trust_level: 5`.

== Aspects

Security:: The cartridges execute untrusted proof and problem files.
Shield tier; every dispatch runs under echidna's sandbox (Podman
`--network=none`, read-only, limits; bwrap fallback; the unsandboxed mode
refused rather than defaulted). ADR-0009's `sandbox-mcp` local provider is
the same mechanism once it exists.
Privacy:: Proof content can be proprietary. The verdict path has no
network egress and no LLM; `echidna-llm-mcp` stays a separate, opt-in
cartridge. Receipts carry hashes, not content.
Honesty:: Every list is read from the capability record. A prover the
host lacks is `UNAVAILABLE` with a reason, never a stub answer. The
store's `available: true` must mean a process answered, which today it
does not for `proof-mcp`.
Licensing:: PVS, Imandra and other non-free backends are dispatchable only
where installed; the cartridge reports them unavailable elsewhere and
never bundles them.
Observability:: Receipts hashed with BLAKE3 as echidna's certificates
are; the kit lock's SHA-256 named per tool so a receipt pins the exact
binary.
Composition:: ADR-0014 applies: `proof-mcp` composing `provers-mcp` is the
first real cross-cartridge call in the store and the right test case for
that ADR.

== Beyond this repository

* `hyperpolymath/boj-server-cartridges`: three new cartridges under
  `domains/formal-verification/`; `proof-mcp` rewired to compose rather
  than POST to port 7707; its backend list replaced by a capability read.
* `hyperpolymath/echidna`: `crates/echidna-mcp` either becomes the
  `provers-mcp` backend contract or is retired under ADR-0002; `list-provers
  --format json` should emit slug, family, binary and tier so the cartridge
  schemas can be rendered; the hard-coded "105" in its tool descriptions
  is the drift `PROVER_COUNT.adoc` names.
* `hyperpolymath/boj-server`: ADR-0014's composition contract gets its
  first instance; the catalogue's `available` flag needs a liveness
  meaning.
* `hyperpolymath/quintessence` and `hyperpolymath/Apodeixis.jl`: a
  `verify --format json` command whose output is the §5 ledger row shape,
  so `code-verifiers-mcp` dispatches to both without adapters.
* `hyperpolymath/atoma`: still the uninitialised RSR template (README
  tokens unfilled); it needs an identity before it can be a verifier row.
* `bactarin`: no repository exists in the estate listing yet.
* `hyperpolymath/standards`: the cartridge spec's domain list may want
  `proving`, `solving` and `code-verification` as named domains rather
  than one `formal-verification`.

== Decisions needed

. Three cartridges (A) or the one-cartridge first step (B) now, growing
  into A.
. Whether `proof-mcp` is kept as the lifecycle layer or retired.
. Whether `crates/echidna-mcp` is retired once `provers-mcp` exists, per
  ADR-0002.
. Whether to open the cartridge scaffolds in `boj-server-cartridges` now
  (experiment Z3) or after the kit's `doctor` ships, since the cartridges'
  honesty depends on the capability record.
